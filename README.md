### Assets used and adaptations

| Project asset | Original source              | Adaptation                                                         |
| ------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `ship.png`    | `Ships Mockup.png`           | Extracted the brown ship with two pale sails and its wake          |
| `cannon.png`  | `Ship Canon 1 Animation.gif` | Arranged its three animation frames into a horizontal sprite sheet |
| `impact.png`  | `Explosion 1.gif`            | Arranged its four animation frames into a horizontal sprite sheet  |

The original solid-blue water colour (`#1C85D9`) was made transparent
in the extracted images so ships, cannons and effects can be layered.
The overlay draws a matching ocean background during playback.

Cannon and impact frames retain their original 150 ms frame timing.
Movement, cannon placement, projectiles and raid-based fleet counts
are implemented by RaidBeacon.

The original asset license notice is preserved at:
`assets/visuals/ships/LICENSE-source.txt`

These credits apply to the third-party artwork. They do not define
the license for RaidBeacon's own source code.
